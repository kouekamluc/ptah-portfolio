01 // Who I Am

My name is Ptah Kouekam Kamgou Luc Kevin. I am an engineering student studying Mechatronics Engineering / Engineering Science at a university in Italy. My work is centered around a clear, multidisciplinary principle: Engineer. Developer. Builder.

I do not approach engineering through narrow specialization or superficial software tricks. Instead, I study and build systems that span the physical and computational realms—from mathematical kinematics and discrete analogue electronics to real-time embedded firmware and production-grade web software.

02 // Physical Engineering & Mechatronics

My core technical discipline is Mechatronics. I study how mechanical structures, electronic circuits, and computational algorithms interact under physical constraints. My projects encompass Newtonian dynamics, mechanical linkages, stress analysis, analogue signal conditioning (operational amplifiers, active Butterworth filters), and classical control theory (PID regulation, transfer functions, stability criteria, and feedback loops).

Whether modeling non-linear atmospheric drag on a physical ballistics rig or analyzing the transient response of an inverted pendulum, I focus on deriving mathematical governing equations first before verifying them on laboratory benches.

03 // Software & Backend Architecture

In software, my primary focus is Python and Django backend development. I build database-driven applications engineered for performance, relational integrity, and long-term maintainability. I prioritize server-side rendering (SSR), ACID transaction modeling, and clean database normalization in PostgreSQL over fragile single-page complexity.

By pairing Django with HTMX and Tailwind CSS, I create reactive, modern user experiences with sub-100ms response times, zero bloated client-side dependencies, and complete accessibility even when JavaScript is disabled.

04 // Embedded Systems & Hardware Integration

Embedded systems serve as the bridge between my mechanical designs and software architectures. I work extensively with the Arduino ecosystem and ESP32 microcontrollers, writing deterministic C/C++ firmware using hardware timer interrupts, FreeRTOS task partitioning, and peripheral buses (SPI, I2C, UART).

My embedded implementations emphasize proper hardware signal conditioning: matching input impedances with operational amplifiers, mitigating electromagnetic noise, and designing reliable data acquisition (DAQ) pipelines for multi-channel sensor streaming.

05 // Building Philosophy

Understand → Model → Build → Measure → Improve

Engineering is an experimental and empirical discipline. I believe in deconstructing problems from first principles, establishing governing differential equations or relational data models, fabricating functional prototypes, capturing objective measurements with instruments or benchmarks, and iteratively eliminating discrepancies between theory and reality.

Current Focus

Advancing my undergraduate engineering curriculum in Italy while developing practical laboratory testbenches, sensor telemetry rigs, and production web software applications. Expanding hands-on mastery across control electronics and Python backend engineering.

Long-Term Direction

Applying integrated mechatronic and software systems to real-world engineering challenges, educational technology, technical media communication, and disciplined product entrepreneurship grounded in verified technical performance.
