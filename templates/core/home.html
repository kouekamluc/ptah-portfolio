04 // Methodology

Engineering Approach

A structured, iterative workflow connecting physical mechanics, circuits, and software.

01

Understand

Deconstruct the physical problem, study boundary conditions, and define measurable specs.

02

Model

Derive governing equations, circuit transfer functions, and software domain models.

03

Build

Fabricate mechanical parts, assemble circuits on breadboards/PCBs, and write firmware/software.

04

Measure

Capture empirical data with oscilloscopes, DAQ logs, step responses, and load benchmarks.

05

Improve

Tune control gains, mitigate electronic noise, refactor architecture, and document insights.
